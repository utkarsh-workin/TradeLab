# TradeLab

## Quantitative Trading & Market Research Platform

TradeLab is a data-driven quantitative trading and market research platform focused on the Indian stock market (NSE/BSE).

The project aims to provide a systematic workflow for:

- Market data collection and validation
- Technical analysis
- Trading strategy development
- Historical backtesting
- Risk and performance analysis
- Out-of-sample validation
- Interactive market research through Streamlit

## V1 Scope

TradeLab V1 focuses on Indian equity markets and includes:

**Data → Technical Analysis → Strategy → Backtesting → Validation → Dashboard**

Advanced capabilities such as US markets, Forex, live trading, automated execution, and real-time alerts are planned for future versions.
